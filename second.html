WELCOME TO THE WORLD OF MY LITTLE SISTER

A POEM FOR HER

From the moment you arrived,so little and so new,
     The whole wide world got brighter,just by having you.
     With cheeks so soft and chubby,and a giggle oh-so-sweet,
     You make the hardest days feel completely obsolete.

     When we take a little walk,your eyes light up with glee,
     At every single furry friend that you might chance to see.
     A puppy with a wagging tail, a dog that stops to play,
     Will bring the biggest, brightest smile to your face, and make your entire day.
     And if a gentle,sleepy cat comes stretching down the steet,
     You watch with total wonder at its little velvet feet.

     But playing takes a lot of work, and soon it’s time to eat!
     For you, the simplest, warmest meals are better than a treat.
     A hearty bowl of rice and daal, cooked with so much love,
     Is the very best dinner that you can’t get enough of.
     And when it’s time to settle down, and the busy day is done,
     A cup of warm and creamy milk is second best to none.

     Yet of all the fancy juices or the drinks that you could pick,
     There is one absolute favorite that does the magic trick.
     A glass of pure, clear water, so refreshing and so cool,
     Is the drink that you love most of all—that is the golden rule!

     You are the tiny princess of our family and our heart,
     And I promise as your brother, we will never drift apart.
     So keep on chasing puppies, and enjoy your rice and daal,
     Because my darling sister, you are the greatest gift of all.

Her Favorite Things

  • Furry Friends: She is the uktimate animal lover! Nothing makes her happier than seeing dogs and cats. A wagging tail or a soft purr is guaranteed to bring out her biggest laughs.
  • Comfort Food Champion: While other kids might cry for candy, her absolute favorite meal is a warm, comforting bowl of Rice and Daal.
  • Milky Way: When she needs a cozy drink, creamy milk is go-to choice.
  • Superpower: Melting everyone's hearts with her adorable giggles and smiles.
